## Ingredients

- 600 grams of AP flour or of your choice
- 420 grams of warm water (tap or filtered is fine)
- 1 packet of instant yeast or 120 grams of active sourdough starter
- 25 grams of honey
- 15 grams of salt 
- Three sprigs of rosemary (ideally fresh)
- A pinch of garlic powder
- A crack of black pepper 
- 100 grams of extra virgin olive oil (I think the costco brand does a great job for recipes like these)

## Steps

1. First measure 420 grams of warm water into your mixing bowl and add your yeast and let sit for a few minutes until you see some bubbles.
2. Next add your honey and stir until fully incoperated into the water
3. Add all seasonings into the bowl that you want to use to flavor your foccacia, garlic powder etc. Mix until fully incoperated into the water.
4. Next add your 600 grams of flour and mix in slowly until homogenous. You should have a ball of sticky but glossy dough. If it is too sticky add more flour if it is too clumpy keep stirring and add water as needed. 
5. Now that you have your dough, transfer it to your baking tin and heavily oil all surfaces and then lightly stretch and fold three times. 
6. Now lightly press on your dough without causing any tears on the surface and stretch it gently as close to the edges of your pan as you can.
7. Now cover and let this rise until it has become nice and airy (ideally filling the pan space).
8. Place your rosemary over the top of the dough as uniformly as you can and drizzle with a bit of olive oil. 
9. Wet your fingers and gently dimple the surface of the dough to give it those nice crunchy pockets. I also like to run my finger along the edges of the pan to help avoid sticking in the bake. 
10. Now prepare a bit of salt water (about a shots worth with a couple pinches of salt will do). Then pour this over the top uniformly. 
11. Place in the oven at 375 and let bake for 20 minutes undisturbed. I recommend rotating 180 degrees after 20 minutes. Then continue the bake until all the water has evaporated and you have a golden brown top crust.
12. Flip onto a wire rack and let cool until it is about body temp (or just rip into it because you have been smelling it the whole time). 

## Notes

- This is a super easy recipe which requires a bit of prep, but ultimately its just mixing things together and waiting. 
- I like to make sandwiches with this which are great for picnics, hikes, a beach day, or even tuesday at work.
