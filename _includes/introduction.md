## Introduction

In a world of ever growing optimization, we have lost sight of our need for human connection and appreciating the little things we have going on. Cooking is at risk of being gobbled up in the AI bubble as well. Many have become too concerned about speedy responses to find a recipe, rather than calling our loved ones. That is likely why you have also come here today. To find something quick and that will help you. 

Cooking a meal is often a daunting task on top of the constant need to be budgeting in our modern world, it has become hard to keep up with everything going on. My goal is to help reduce this stress for you by providing easy, affordable, and sometimes healthy recipes that will brighten up your day. 

My name is Paco Navarro, I am a phycisist by trade, but I have worked in the restaurant industry basically my whole life and this is my guide to cooking. 

## Before You Start

This is a series of recipes I have compiled over the years both through my time budgeting in grad school, working in restaurants, and most importantly learning from friends and family. I am also trying to use items that you would probably already have laying around the house. 

In future versions, once the website is more filled out, I will include a large list of common pantry items and tool that I think everyone should have available.
